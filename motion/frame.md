# Делопуск — funnel frame sequence

## Frame 01 — Landing

- Dark editorial hero.
- Main promise: `Откройте ИП. Получите AI‑команду.`
- Three visible access states: `5 сейчас`, `+12 после заявки`, `Pro после подтверждения`.
- AI workspace appears after the promise and primary CTA.

### Entrance

- 0–160 ms: brand and navigation.
- 120–600 ms: badge, headline and body.
- 260–760 ms: actions and access chips.
- 380–980 ms: AI workspace layers with 70–100 ms stagger.

## Frame 02 — AI capabilities

- Light background.
- Four ready modules and two beta modules.
- Real product surfaces instead of generic decorative cards.
- Status labels remain visible without hover.

## Frame 03 — Access ladder

- Dark background.
- Four-stage route:
  1. five trial actions;
  2. twelve additional actions after application;
  3. Pro after confirmed IP and RKO;
  4. thirty-day work period.
- Counters and progress lines are descriptive, not artificial urgency.

## Frame 04 — Pro bundle

- Light background.
- Exact bundle checklist on the left.
- Editable AI-package dashboard on the right.
- Primary CTA leads to the IP bridge.

## Frame 05 — Segments

- Dark background.
- Marketplace, services, local and B2B entry points.
- Every segment advertises the five-action trial and leads to the same quiz.

## Frame 06 — Final CTA

- Light outer section and dark inner panel.
- Copy: try AI first, unlock more after application.
- Three access states repeat beside the CTA.

## Quiz

- Access counter is always visible.
- One question per screen.
- One action is consumed only after successful generation.
- Zero limit shows the partner bridge instead of a generic error.

## Result

- Personal AI output remains the dominant surface.
- Access card is secondary but visible above the fold.
- Ladder repeats `5 → +12 → Pro`.
- Partner CTA explains what opens next.

## IP Bridge

- Official partner link is the primary action.
- The page explains that passport data stays with the partner.
- After application the user returns and activates the temporary +12 bonus.
- Pro is described as conditional on confirmed IP registration and RKO.

## Access motion

- Counter change: 420 ms.
- Card surface change: 560 ms.
- Pro unlock: 700 ms with a single soft glow.
- No confetti, shaking, fake countdown or forced auto-scroll.

## Mobile

- Preserve the same semantic order.
- Stack access stages vertically.
- Keep the current remaining actions visible near the primary control.
- Use a sticky partner CTA only after the result or when the trial limit is zero.
